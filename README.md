# Emission_Powerbi_dashboard
Analysis of Emission data reported by companies from 2021-2023


## Overview
- This Power BI dashboard analyzes emission trends from 2021 to 2023, identifying key contributors, regional patterns, and year-over-year changes.

## Objectives
- Analyze emission trends over time
- Identify top 10 emitters
- Compare emissions across regions and categories
- Highlight key insights for decision-making
  
## Dataset
- Source:  Emission dataset
- Years Covered: 2021–2023

## Key Insights
- Emissions peaked in 2021 and showed partial decline in 2023
- Top 10 companies contribute the majority of emissions
- 2023 data may be incomplete due to fewer reporting companies
- Significant variation observed across regions
- Asia pacific companies are top emitter.


## Tools Used
- Power BI
- Excel

  
## Features
- Interactive slicers
- KPI cards
- Trend analysis
- Top N filtering

⚠️ Note
2023 data may not fully represent actual emissions due to limited reporting.
